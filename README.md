## Case 1 - Delivery Performance & Review Risk

E-commerce / logistics. 95,824 delivered orders, Jan 2017 - Aug 2018.

Late delivery is the single biggest driver of 1-star reviews: 46.2% of late orders get 1 star versus 6.6% of on-time orders, a 7x difference. $484K of revenue exposed, $397K recoverable with a 3-day buffer. 87% of the delay traced to the carrier.

Dashboard: https://voymart.github.io/portfolio/olist.html

Report: https://voymart.github.io/portfolio/olist_findings_report.pdf

## Case 2 - Discount Policy & Profit Leakage

US retail superstore. 9,994 order lines, 5,009 orders, 2014-2017.

Every discount above 20% loses money. Those lines are only 16% of revenue but destroy $135K of profit, against a company-wide profit of $286K. Capping discounts at 20% would raise profit by 62%, about $179K, without selling one extra unit.

Dashboard: https://voymart.github.io/portfolio/superstore.html

Report: https://voymart.github.io/portfolio/superstore_findings_report.pdf
