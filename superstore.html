Discount Policy & Profit Leakage

US retail superstore · 9,994 order lines · 5,009 orders · 2014–2017
Headline finding

Every discount above 20% loses money. Those lines are only 16% of revenue but destroy $135K of profit — against a company-wide profit of $286K. Capping discounts at 20% would raise profit by 62% without touching a single price below that line.

What to do about it

1
Cap discounts at 20% and require approval above it
There is no discount band above 20% with a positive margin. At 30% the margin is already −10%; at 70% it is −99%. This is a policy change, not a pricing study.
+$179K profit
2
Fix Tables, Bookcases and Machines before touching anything else
Tables alone is $207K of revenue at a −8.6% margin, discounted 26% on average. Machines carries a 31% average discount for a 1.8% margin. Three sub-categories account for the bulk of the leak.
$396K revenue in scope
3
Audit the Central region, not the products
Central averages a 24% discount and returns a 7.9% margin. The West averages 11% and returns 14.9% on more revenue. Same catalogue, different discipline.
7 points of margin gap
