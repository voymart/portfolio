Delivery Performance & Review Risk

Brazilian marketplace · 95,824 delivered orders · Jan 2017 – Aug 2018
 
Headline finding

Late delivery is the single biggest driver of 1-star reviews. 46.2% of late orders get 1 star, versus 6.6% of on-time orders — a 7× difference. The damage is concentrated in 8.0% of orders and carries $484K of revenue.

What to do about it

1
Add a 3-day buffer to the promised delivery date
The cliff sits at day 3, not day 0. Padding the promise converts most 4–7 day failures into 1–3 day ones, where buyers barely react.
~2,485 fewer 1-star reviews
2
Audit the carriers, not the sellers
Seller handling adds 2.7 extra days on late orders. Carrier transit adds 17.8. 8.7% of all orders spend more than 20 days in transit.
Root cause of 87% of the delay
3
Flag orders at day 3 past promise and contact the buyer first
Once an order crosses the cliff the review is effectively already lost. A proactive message before the buyer complains is the only lever left.
5,025 orders/period in scope
